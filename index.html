hey, i'm Laasya, a sophomore studying neuroscience + entrepreneurship at UT Austin. i'm working towards building an integrated neurotechnology innovation and adoption ecosystem that spans clinical care, research, development, and venture capital to advance the frontier of pediatric neurological care.

most of my time right now is split between researching and developing non-invasive brain computer interfaces & neuromodulatory assistive technologies for populations with neurodevelopmental and neurodegenerative conditions, working at a pediatric neurology clinic, building my pediatric neurotech wearable startup, scouting for medtech/digital health VCs, working at a few medical and consumer neurotech startups, organizing pop-up pediatric neuro clinics for underserved communities, and hosting IRL events across austin, sf & nyc to bring patient-centric researchers, founders, healthcare providers, advocates & investors into rooms that shape the future of neurotechnology, especially for pediatric applications.

outside of neurotech, I'm also an equally avid and passionate social entrepreneur working to reform systems that enable intergenerational trauma and socioeconomic and humanitarian injustices through building infrastructure for entrepreneurial upward mobility. through identifying & reforming the suppressive, dysregulated, and destructive forces of our minds and society, I hope to create an impact that enables humanity's fair pursuit of peace, opportunity, and happiness for generations to come.
