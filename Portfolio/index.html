hey — i'm laasya. sophomore studying neuroscience at UT Austin with an entrepreneurship minor. i'm building toward one long-term thing: a vertically integrated, patient-centric neurotechnology innovation ecosystem — spanning clinical care, research, product development, regulatory advocacy, venture capital, and an accelerator — that lets neurodivergent kids live independent, safe, fulfilling lives.
